My Approach

How I think through problems.

I try to keep problem-solving simple: understand the question, find the right information, identify what matters, and turn it into a clear next step.

01
Structure
Start by figuring out what actually needs to be solved and breaking the problem into manageable pieces.
02
Data
Find the right information, check the assumptions behind it, and make sure the analysis is built on something reliable.
03
Insight
Look beyond the numbers to identify patterns that actually matter to the business and the decision being made.
04
Recommend
Turn the analysis into a clear recommendation that explains what should happen next and why.
