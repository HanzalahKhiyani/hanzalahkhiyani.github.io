My Approach

How I think through problems.

Every engagement starts with the same discipline: understand the actual problem before solving the apparent one.

01
Structure
Decompose ambiguity into a clear problem statement and a decision framework that actually holds under pressure.
02
Data
Identify the right inputs, build from reliable sources, and pressure-test every assumption before moving forward.
03
Insight
Surface patterns that are business-relevant, not just statistically interesting — and know the difference between the two.
04
Recommend
Deliver a clear point of view with a defensible path forward. Analysis without a recommendation is unfinished work.
