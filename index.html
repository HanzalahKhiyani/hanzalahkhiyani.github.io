My Approach

How I think through problems.

Every engagement starts with the same discipline: understand the actual problem before solving the apparent one. My approach as an analytics-first consultant follows a consistent four-step model:

1. Structure — Decompose ambiguity into a clear problem statement and a decision framework that actually holds.

2. Data — Identify the right inputs, build from reliable sources, and pressure-test every assumption before moving forward.

3. Insight — Surface patterns that are business-relevant, not just statistically interesting — and know the difference.

4. Recommend — Deliver a clear point of view with a defensible path forward. Analysis without a recommendation is unfinished work.
